Step by Step Guide

Unblock Karne Ka
Asaan Tareeqa

Bas 5 simple steps... promise! 🤞

1

Instagram App Kholein 📱

Apna phone uthao, Instagram ka icon dhoondo (wo gradient wala — purple, pink, orange) aur tap karo. Simple sa kaam hai na? 😄

2

Profile Pe Jayein 👤

Neeche right corner mein apna profile picture dikhega — uspe tap karo. Ab tum apne profile page pe ho. Easy peasy! ✨

3

Menu (☰) Kholein

Profile pe upar right side mein teen lines (☰) ka button hoga — uspe tap karo. Ek menu khulega jismein bahut saare options honge. Daro mat! 😊

4

"Your Activity" Pe Tap Karein 📊

Menu mein neechay scroll karo, "Your activity" naam ka option milega. Wo dhundo aur tap karo. Yeh tumhari saari activity ka hub hai! 🔍

5

"Blocked Accounts" → Unblock! 🎉

"Your activity" mein "Blocked accounts" pe tap karo. Wo list dikhegi. Wahaan se uss banday (jo bohot cute hai 😉) ka naam dhondo aur "Unblock" pe tap karo. Bas — ho gaya! 🥳

Confirm karne ka button bhi aayega — uspe bhi "Unblock" tap karo. Done! 💜
